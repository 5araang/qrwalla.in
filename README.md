# QRWALLA (`qrwalla.in`) — Free Static & Dynamic QR Code Platform

> **QR codes. Made simple.**  
> 100% Free Forever • Zero Advertisements • No Expiration • High-Res PNG & Vector SVG Exports

---

## Overview

**QRWALLA** (`https://qrwalla.in`) is a fast, modern, free QR code platform for creating customizable **Static QR Codes**, **Dynamic QR Codes**, and **Smart App Opener** deep links—all in one clean workspace.

---

## Core Features

### 1. Static QR Code Generator
Generate instant, offline-ready QR codes stored directly in the QR matrix with zero redirect servers required:
- **Website & URL** — Direct visitors to any website, landing page, portfolio, or store.
- **WhatsApp Click-to-Chat** — Start 1-on-1 conversations with an optional pre-filled message.
- **Email & Inquiry** — Open the scanner's mail app with pre-filled recipient, subject, and body.
- **Social Profiles** — Link directly to Instagram, YouTube, X (Twitter), LinkedIn, or TikTok.
- **Digital vCard (Contact Card)** — Save contact details, phone numbers, company, and email straight to a phone's address book in one scan.
- **Wi-Fi, Plain Text & PDF Links** — Share network access, offline notes, or downloadable documents effortlessly.

### 2. Dynamic QR Code Management
Create editable short-link QR codes that let you update the destination URL anytime without reprinting physical materials:
- **Editable Destination URL** — Change where your printed QR code points whenever campaigns, menus, or links change.
- **Custom Short Slugs** — Choose clean, memorable short links (`qrwalla.in/r/qr/your-slug`) with real-time availability checking.
- **Real-Time Scan Analytics** — Track total scans, unique visitors, daily scan velocity, device breakdown (Mobile, Desktop, Tablet), and top referrers.
- **Pause or Resume Anytime** — Activate or pause dynamic QR codes instantly from the dashboard.

### 3. Smart App Opener (Deep Link Generator)
Bypass restrictive in-app WebView browsers (Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp, LinkedIn) and open links directly inside native iOS & Android apps:
- Supports **YouTube**, **Instagram**, **Spotify**, **LinkedIn**, **X / Twitter**, **Telegram**, **WhatsApp**, and **Snapchat**.
- Increases conversions, app engagement, and subscriber actions by launching the native mobile app automatically.

### 4. Custom QR Design & High-Res Exports
- **Custom Patterns & Corner Eyes** — Classic square, rounded dots, extra-rounded modules, and custom finder eyes.
- **Brand Colors & Frames** — Custom foreground/background palettes, transparent backgrounds, and call-to-action frames (`SCAN ME`).
- **Center Logo Overlay** — Upload your brand icon or choose built-in platform badges with automatic error-correction (`H` level) for reliable scanning.
- **Print-Ready Downloads** — Export crisp `512px`, `1024px`, or `2048px` **PNG** files and infinite-resolution **Vector SVG** files.
